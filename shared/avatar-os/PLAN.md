## Training plan

Anchors: Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, Friday. Sunday is an optional fifth session
and is skipped during travel or a bad week without penalty. Wednesday and
Saturday are recovery days.

Use a completion-based A/B rotation. Advance to the other routine only when a
session is completed. A skipped session creates no debt.

### Workout A — push and legs, 25–35 minutes

1. Push-ups — 3 controlled sets, stop 1–2 reps before form failure
2. Bench press or dumbbell press — 3 × 8–12
3. Goblet squat — 3 × 8–12
4. Lateral raise — 2 × 12–15
5. Plank — 2 controlled sets

### Workout B — pull and legs, 25–35 minutes

1. Lat pulldown or assisted pull-up — 3 × 8–12
2. Row — 3 × 8–12
3. Romanian deadlift — 3 × 8–12
4. Curl — 2 × 10–15
5. Dead bug or plank — 2 controlled sets

Warm up briefly for the movements being trained. Rest roughly 60–90 seconds.
When all prescribed sets reach the top of the rep range with sound form for two
sessions, make the smallest available load increase. Adherence and safe form
matter more than load.
